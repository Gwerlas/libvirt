Working on gwerlas.libvirt
==========================

Everything a contributor needs is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), which
links the engineering handbook and the Ansible family guide that bind this
project. Two of its sections govern what you *write* rather than what you
change, and they are the ones most easily skipped:

- **Where a rationale lives** — which of a code comment, `docs/`, a commit
  message or a merge request holds a given reason; the handbook page it links
  holds the deletion tests that decide. Run them on what you produced, before
  calling a change ready.
- **Submit your changes** — a change arrives with its scenario and its
  documentation in the same commit, and an issue is referenced from the commit
  body and nowhere else.

No rule is restated here, on purpose: a second copy of a rule is a rule that
will diverge from the first.
